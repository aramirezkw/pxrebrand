KWIQ Button Explorations

Thirteen treatments in the glow-and-gradient family — conic rings, ambient bloom, frosted glass, light pooled under the face. The reference board is doing one thing consistently: it makes the button look like a light source rather than a shape, so the surface around it reacts.

Each sits in a live Command bar at true scale, built from the shipped mark. Hover any specimen; the strip underneath shows rest, hover and pressed together. Most of these stay inside KW cyan — a few reach for indigo and violet so you can see where the family stops being ours.

Where I’d take it

The read

This whole family works by spending light, and a 64px bar gives you very little room to spend it in. The three that survive that constraint are 02, 03 and 07 — all single-hue, all cyan, all legible at 36px without a gradient doing the explaining.

02 is the one I’d put in front of the team. It is the reference board’s idea executed entirely in KW cyan: the glow is the brand color, not a borrowed spectrum, and it reads as “powered” rather than “decorated”.

11 is the useful compromise: 10’s nebula clipped into a normal 36px control, so you can put that treatment in the bar without giving up the corner. Worth building purely to see whether the effect survives being small.

Where the family breaks

01, 06 and 12 are the most faithful to the reference and the least faithful to KW — a rotating spectrum on a bar that already carries KW Red gives the chrome three competing identities. Beautiful in isolation, loud in situ, and 12 is the loudest of the three because a pill gives the gradient the most arc to show.

04, 10 and 13 bring indigo and violet into a system whose action color is teal. Worth showing to make the boundary visible, not worth shipping unless PX is deliberately extending the palette — which is a brand decision, not a button decision.

What to resolve

  1. Does the glow survive the light dashboard below it, or does it only work against #22262A? Test 03 and 07 on both surfaces.
  2. Pick the motion budget. 01, 06, 09 and 10 animate at rest — that is a permanent moving object in the chrome, and it needs a reduced-motion answer.
  3. 09’s ping should fire on new insight, not on a loop. Decide what triggers it before it becomes decoration.
  4. Glow is not a token yet. Whatever wins needs a named elevation in XDS, or every team re-invents the blur radius.
